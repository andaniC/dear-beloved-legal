Dear Beloved

Privacy Policy

Effective 30 September 2026

In short: what you share with Dear Beloved is used to write your letters and keep them safe, and for nothing else. There are no ads, no tracking and no selling of your information. You can delete your account and everything in it from the app at any time.

Who we are

Dear Beloved is made by Andani Mukwevho, an independent developer in South Africa (“we”, “us”). We are responsible for your personal information under South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). You can reach us at mukwevhoandani1020@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • What you tell the app. Your first name; what is on your heart (the topics you choose and anything you write in your own words); how your heart has been lately; what you most need to hear; where you are with God; and when you want your letters to arrive.
  • Your letters. The letters written for you, which ones you have opened, saved or deleted, and what you write back to God.
  • Your account. The app creates an account for you automatically, identified by a random ID. If you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple gives us an identifier and an email address (a private relay address if you pick “Hide My Email”), and your name the first time you sign in.
  • Your subscription. If you subscribe to Dear Beloved Plus, Apple handles the payment and we never see your card details. RevenueCat tells the app whether your subscription is active, linked to your random account ID.
  • Basic technical records. Our servers keep short-lived logs of requests (such as the time and IP address) for security and troubleshooting.

Some of what you share, such as your faith and what you are going through, may be special personal information. We only process it because you choose to share it so that your letters can speak to it, and you can change or remove it at any time in Settings.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t show ads, and we don’t sell or rent your information.
  • We don’t track you across other apps or websites, and the app contains no analytics or advertising tools.
  • Letter notifications are scheduled on your phone itself; we don’t send push notifications from a server.

How your letters are written

Your letters are written by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. When a letter is written for you, our server sends Anthropic:

  • the answers described above (your topics, your own words, how your heart has been, what you need to hear and where you are with God);
  • which of the day’s letters it is and its date, and the headlines and Bible references of your recent letters, so letters don’t repeat;
  • if you wrote back to God, what you wrote (up to 1,500 characters) and the headline of the letter you were answering.

Your name is not sent: letters are written with a placeholder that the app fills in on your phone. Anthropic uses this information only to write the letter, under terms that do not allow it to be used to train its models.

Who helps us run Dear Beloved

  • Supabase hosts our database and servers in Frankfurt, Germany. It stores your account and the backup of your letters.
  • Anthropic (United States) writes your letters, as described above.
  • RevenueCat (United States) manages Plus subscriptions.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple, handles payments and distributes the app.

They process your information only to provide their service to us. We may also disclose information if the law requires it. Because these providers are outside South Africa, your information is transferred abroad; we rely on their contractual commitments to protect it.

On your phone

Your letters and answers are also kept on your phone, so the app works offline. If you add the Dear Beloved widget, it shows your name and today’s letter on your Home Screen or Lock Screen.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as you have an account. When you delete a letter from your Mailbox, it is hidden everywhere and anything you wrote back to it is removed. To delete everything, use Settings → Delete account in the app: your account and all of its information are deleted from our servers straight away, and from your phone. Copies may remain in our hosting provider’s backups for a short time until they are overwritten. RevenueCat and Apple keep records of purchases as required for billing and the law.

Keeping it safe

Information is encrypted in transit. Each reader’s data is protected so that only their own account can read it, and the keys that reach Anthropic and our database never leave our servers.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete your personal information, object to how we use it, or withdraw your consent, by writing to mukwevhoandani1020@gmail.com. Much of this you can also do yourself in the app. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you may complain to South Africa’s Information Regulator, or to the data protection authority where you live.

Children

Dear Beloved is for anyone aged 13 or older. If you are under 18, please check with a parent or guardian before using it. We don’t knowingly collect information from children under 13; if you believe a child under 13 has used the app, contact us and we will delete their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update it here and change the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also let you know in the app.
